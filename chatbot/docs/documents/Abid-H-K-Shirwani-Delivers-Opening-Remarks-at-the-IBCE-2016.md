# News and Announcement
## Abid H K Shirwani Delivers Opening Remarks at the IBCE 2016
Monday, November 07, 2016
**Abid H K Shirwani** , Director General UMT and Chief Organizer IBCE, welcomed the national and international delegates, and distinguished guests. He said that educational institutes seldom organize events to promote trade and investment. In this regard. UMT has scored a first by organizing the IBCE 2016 that aims to create s synergy in order to move the development agenda forward and add value to Pakistani products. He said that with the help of our Chinese and Turkish brothers, the IBCE is all set to drive technology transfer, facilitate innovation and boost entrepreneurship.
![](http://admin.umt.edu.pk/Media/Site/UMT/FileManager/2016/ibce/ibce-highlights27.jpg) ![](http://admin.umt.edu.pk/Media/Site/UMT/FileManager/2016/ibce/ibce-highlights28.jpg)
The DG UMT took the opportunity to thank the 50+ foreign delegates and 150 companies that are participating in the IBCE 2016. He also thanked the sponsors, media channels, especially the Jang Media Group which is the media partner for the event. He said that UMT has decided to organize IBCE 2016 regularly- ‘I invite you to attend the IBCE 2017 and beyond.’
The UMT International Business Conference and Exhibition (IBCE) kicked off to an impressive start at the Lahore Expo Center. With exhibitors from China and Turkey, plus leading business groups of Pakistan in attendance, the IBCE 2016 showcased new and innovative products, technologies and services. The opening session attracted business gurus, entrepreneurs and CEOs from leading national and international companies. Countless business networking opportunities, a rare convergence of buyers, investors, suppliers and technology partners turned the IBCE 2016 into one of the most anticipated events of the year.
