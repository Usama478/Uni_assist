_**Guidelines regarding Semester Freeze:**_


 Participant may apply for semester Freeze for medical or other plausible reasons (duly supported by

documents) to the Office of Registrar (ORG) at least fifteen days before the Add/Drop period for approval.
 The right of the participant to resume studies immediately after this period is taken for granted, without any

additional dues. In such cases, the participant should apply to Office of Registrar (ORG), at least 15 days
before the commencement of the semester.
 Failure to resume studies after the approved Semester Leave period may result into cancellation of

admission. However, a letter of warning shall precede admission cancellation.
 First Semester cannot be frozen.
