## How to Apply
There are two methods for applying into the degree programs offered at UMT Sialkot Campus:
  * **Submit Form at OIA, UMT Sialkot  
**


The admissions kit is available from the Office of Information and Admissions at UMT. Follow the guidelines mentioned below:
## Admission Guidelines
  * Get the Admission Kit that is available with the Office of Admissions for Rs 2000/-
  * Outstation candidates can receive the Admission Kit by sending a bank draft/money order of Rs 2000/- in favor of UMT Sialkot
  * Fill the Application Form provided with the Kit
  * Submit the Form on or before the deadline
  * Enclose the following material with the Form 
    * Eight photographs of Passport size duly attested from back side with name mentioned on each picture 
    * Attach the remaining photographs with the Application Form
    * Photocopy of National Identity Card
    * Photocopy of Father's National Identity Card
    * Domicile of the candidate or parent's domicile 
    * Copies of academic records including mark sheets, degrees, certificates, diplomas, etc., duly attested by a gazetted officer or equivalent
    * Provisional Certificate from the head of the institution, in case result is awaited
    * Copy of the Roll No. Slip and a Provisional Certificate from a gazetted officer, in case of a private candidate
    * Equivalence certificate from the Higher Education Commission of Pakistan in case of applicants with foreign qualifications
    * Experience Certificate (if applicable)
