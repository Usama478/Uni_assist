Guidelines Regarding Semester Leave:


 Participant may apply for semester leave up to two semesters for medical or other plausible reasons (duly

supported by documents) to the Office of the Registrar at least fifteen days before the Add/Drop period for
approval.
 The right of the participant to resume studies immediately after this period is taken for granted, without any

additional dues. In such cases, the participant should apply to Office of the Registrar, at least 15 days before the
commencement of the semester.
 Failure to resume studies after the approved Semester Leave period may result into cancellation of admission.

However, a letter of warning shall precede admission cancellation.
