# Academic Programs
## Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
[Overview](http://skt.umt.edu.pk/Programs/Undergraduate-Programs/Bachelor-of-Business-Administration-\(BBA\).aspx) [Road Map](http://skt.umt.edu.pk/Programs/Undergraduate-Programs/Bachelor-of-Business-Administration-\(BBA\)/BBA-Road-Map.aspx)
**Introduction**
The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) is a four years’ degree program that offers the fundamental concepts required in today's business world. The program is tailored around the basic areas of business, management, economics, marketing, accounting, and finance. It trains students to develop an understanding and appreciation of the global business environment. Furthermore, the students will be guided by high ethical standards to operate in the local business environment. A distinctive feature of the BBA program is that it incorporates within the curriculum, major emphasis on the development of students' fundamental learning skills, for example: reasoning and quantitative abilities; as well as communication and computing skills which they will need for responsible leadership roles in their careers.
**Vision**
We prepare our students to become the future ethical business leaders and the guiding post for the society, while equipping them with the knowledge and skills required to become world-class professionals. We will be the leading choice for organizations seeking highly talented human resource. KUBEAC will foster internationalization with key stakeholders and actively work to exchange best practices with business schools across the region through collaborations, workshops, conferences and other means.
**Mission**
At KUBEAC, we are committed to serve the community by developing leadership, professional and entrepreneurial skills of the students. We strive to achieve it by encouraging our faculty to employ diverse teaching methodologies and providing state of the art learning environment to the students**.**
**Program Objectives**
The KUBEAC objectives specify the intellectual and behavioural competencies that graduates should possess and that provide a foundation for their future professional and personal development and success. Learning goals are general statements, so a number of measurable program objectives are also established for each goal.
**1.****Effective Communication Skills**
**Learning Outcome:** Graduates must be able to draft effective business documents and prepare and deliver effective oral business presentations by using a variety of appropriate technologies.
**2.****Problem Solving and Reasoning Skills**
**Learning Outcome:** Graduates must be able to reflect thinking techniques to identify and analyse problems, develop viable alternatives, make effective decisions and apply appropriate quantitative and qualitative techniques to solve business problems.
**3. Team work and Leadership Roles**
**Learning Outcome:** Graduates must be able to work effectively in teams and understand group processes, leadership, conflict, power and culture in organizations.
**4. Core Business Knowledge and Capabilities**
**Learning Outcome:** Graduate must be able to demonstrate competency in the underlying concepts, theory and tools taught in the core undergraduate curriculum. Moreover, graduates must be instilled with the required skills to initiate independent ventures or to upgrade existing businesses through effective decision making and exploiting untapped potential within the market.
**5.****Ethics in Doing Businesses**
**Learning Outcome:** Graduates must be able to identify and analyse ethical conflicts and issues involving different stakeholders in order to develop viable alternatives and make effective decisions relating to business ethics.
**6.****Industry Focus**
**Learning Outcome:** Graduates must be able to understand the dynamics of local industry and understand business as an integrated system and apply strategic planning tools to coordinate among the functional areas. 
**7. Global Perspective**
**Learning Outcome** : Graduates must be able to identify and analyse relevant global factors that influence decision-making in their respective domains and develop viable alternatives and solutions for international business settings. They must also be able to identify and assess relevant global practices that influence work performance in different functional areas and to plan, redesign and implement those practices in the local business context.
**8.****Social Responsibility**
**Learning Outcome:** Graduate must be socially responsible in their professions and business settings.

  * 12-years of formal education with minimum 50% marks/ A-Level.
  * 50% Marks in NTS/UMT Entry Test.

  * Semesters: 8
  * Credit Hours: 126

## **Program Structure**
**Semester I**
  * Business Mathematics
  * Micro Economics
  * Fundamental of Accounting
  * English Grammar & Comprehension
  * Introduction to Business
  * Pakistan Development & Reconstruction
  * English Immersion-1


**Semester II**
  * Islamic Perspective in Business
  * Computer Applications in Business
  * Business Law
  * Cost Accounting
  * Composition & Communication
  * Business Statistics
  * 21st Century Skills
  * First Summer-Corporate Social Responsibility (I Credit Hour)


**Semester III**
  * Introduction to Psychology
  * Life and Learning
  * Statistical Inferences
  * Research Paper Writing & Presentation
  * Principles Of Management
  * Management Information Systems


**Semester IV**
  * Decision Models
  * English for Specific Purposes (Business)
  * Business Finance
  * Organizational Behavior
  * Principles of Marketing
  * Business Research and Intelligence
  * Second Summer-International/National Tour (I Credit Hour)


**Semester V**
  * Production operations Management
  * Macro Economics
  * Financial Management
  * Leadership Skills
  * Marketing Management


**Semester VI**
  * E-Business
  * Entrepreneurship
  * Development Economics
  * Managing Human Capital
  * Introduction to Sociology
  * Internship (0 Credit Hour) 


**Semester VII**
  * Foreign Language
  * Major Elective I
  * Major Elective II
  * Minor Elective I
  * English Immersion-3


**Semester VIII**
  * Business Strategy
  * Major Elective III
  * Industry/Research Project OR Minor Elective II


**Internship**
Students will undertake 6-8 weeks internship in a company.
