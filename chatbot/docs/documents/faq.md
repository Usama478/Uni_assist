## 2. Admission & Eligibility Criteria (2025-2026)

**Q: What is the basic eligibility for BS Programs?**
A: generally, you need at least **45% to 50% marks** in Intermediate (FA/FSc/ICS/I.Com) or A-Levels.
* **Engineering Programs:** 60% marks in FSc (Pre-Engineering) + UMT Entry Test.
* **CS/IT/SE:** 50% marks in ICS/FSc (Pre-Medical students must pass deficiency math courses).
* **Arts/Social Sciences:** 45% marks in Intermediate.

**Q: How is the Merit calculated for Undergraduate admissions?**
A: The merit is usually calculated as follows:
* **Matriculation:** 20% weightage
* **Intermediate:** 50% weightage
* **Entrance Test/Interview:** 30% weightage

**Q: Does UMT accept NTS/NAT tests?**
A: Yes, UMT accepts NTS-NAT results. Alternatively, you can take the internal UMT entrance test.

## 4. Scholarships & Financial Aid

**Q: Does UMT Sialkot offer Merit Scholarships for BS students?**
A: Yes, based on Intermediate/Intermediate Equivalent marks:
* **Position Holders:** 100% Waiver + Stipend
* **95% and above:** 100% Tuition Waiver
* **90% - 94.99%:** 90% Waiver
* **85% - 89.99%:** 80% Waiver
* **80% - 84.99%:** 50% Waiver
* **75% - 79.99%:** 35% Waiver
* **70% - 74.99%:** 25% Waiver

**Q: What is the Kinship Policy (Siblings)?**
A: If two or more siblings are studying at UMT simultaneously, the second sibling typically receives a tuition fee discount (often around 15-20% depending on current policy).

**Q: What are the criteria to *maintain* a scholarship in subsequent semesters?**
A: You must maintain a specific CGPA to keep your scholarship:
* To keep **100%** scholarship: Maintain **3.90 - 4.00 CGPA**.
* To keep **50%** scholarship: Maintain **3.50 CGPA**.
* To keep **25%** scholarship: Maintain **3.25 CGPA**.
* *Note: If your CGPA drops, your scholarship percentage decreases.*

## 5. Academic Policies & Grading

**Q: What grading system does UMT use?**
A: UMT uses a **Relative Grading System** (Bell Curve). Your grade depends on your performance relative to the class average (Mean) and Standard Deviation.

**Q: What is the minimum attendance required?**
A: Students must maintain **75% to 80% attendance** in every course.
* Falling below this threshold typically results in an **"SA" (Short Attendance)** grade, meaning you fail the course and must retake it.

**Q: What is an "I" Grade?**
A: "I" stands for **Incomplete**. It is awarded only in extreme emergencies (like severe illness) if approved by the Dean. You must complete the remaining work in the next semester, or it turns into an "F".

**Q: What is the passing GPA?**
* **Undergraduate:** Minimum CGPA of **2.00** is required to graduate.
* **Graduate (MS/PhD):** Minimum CGPA of **2.50** (sometimes 3.00 for PhD) is required.

## 6. Campus Facilities

**Q: Does UMT Sialkot provide transport?**
A: Yes, a shuttle service is available for students covering major routes in Sialkot, Daska, Sambrial, and Gujranwala.
* **Requirement:** You must carry your valid Student ID card to board the bus.
* **Charges:** Transport fees are usually charged per semester.

**Q: Are there hostels available?**
A: Yes, UMT Sialkot has separate hostels for male and female students.
* Facilities include mess (food), WiFi, and laundry.
* Accommodation is usually on a first-come, first-served basis.

**Q: How do I access the Library?**
A: You can access the library using your Student ID card. The library offers physical books, journals, and access to HEC Digital Library resources.

## 7. Digital Services (LMS & Portals)

**Q: What is the LMS link for Sialkot Campus?**
A: The Learning Management System (Moodle) is located at: `https://sktlms.umt.edu.pk/`
* Use this for: Assignments, Quizzes, Lecture Slides, and Course Announcements.

**Q: What is the Student Portal link?**
A: The participant portal is usually at: `https://online.umt.edu.pk/` or accessed via the main site.
* Use this for: Checking Fee Challans, Attendance records, and Transcripts.

**Q: How do I reset my LMS password?**
A: If you forget your password, visit the IPC (Information Processing Center) help desk on campus or use the "Forgot Password" link on the LMS login page if your recovery email is set.

**Q: How do I connect to Campus WiFi?**
A: Students can connect to the official student WiFi network using their **Student ID** and **Password** (same credentials as your portal/LMS).
