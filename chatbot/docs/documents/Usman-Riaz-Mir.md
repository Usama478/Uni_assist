## Usman Riaz Mir
MS (MGT) UMT Lahore
Assistant Professor
usman.riaz@skt.umt.edu.pk
**MS Thesis Supervised:**
  * Impact of organizational commitment and job satisfaction on Turnover Intentions: A case of Banking Industry.
  * Role of organizational spirituality for enhancing employee performance with moderating effect of ethical behavior.
  * Impact of psychological contract for knowledge management practices with moderating effect of organizational culture.
  * Enhancing team performance through new knowledge creation and organizational learning.


**Conference Papers:**
  * Mir, U. R. (2014). New Knowledge Creation and Crisis management team’s performance. In ELLTA Malaysia Conference.
  * Mir, U. R. (2014). An Islamic perspective of CSR. In ICOBM Conference.
  * Mir, U. R. (2014). Effect of caste on entrepreneurial abilities. In ICOBM Conference.
  * Mir, U. R. (2014). An ethical rationale for adopting CSR: An Islamic Perspective. In AGBA-SAICON Conference.
  * Mir, U. R. (2015). Islamic work ethics: An antecedent for sustainable motivation and effective employee. In CIFB FAST Conference.
  * Mir, U. R. (2015). Higher Purpose and Performance through workplace Spirituality. In AGBA- SAICON Conference.
  * Mir, U. R. (2016). Impact of Assessment and learning tools on students learning effectiveness: A case of distance learning education. In ELDEC Conference.
  * Mir, U.R & Ain, Q.U. (2016). Are Job satisfaction and Organizational Commitment still valid predictors of Turnover Intention? In ICOBM2016.
  * Mir, U. R. (2017). A spiritual perspective on organizational purpose and performance. In Academy of Management Conference 2017 (AOM2017), America.


**HEC Recognized Journal Articles:**
  * Mir, U. R., Sair, S. A., Malik, M. E. (2014). Effects of Organizational culture and Top management support on ERP implementation. Science International, 26(3), 1361-1369.
  * Mir, U. R., Sair, S. A. (2014). Ethical Rationale of adopting CSR: An Islamic Perspective. Science International, 26(5), 2353-2360.

  * Hassan,S.M, Mir, U.R, Hassan, S.S. (2015). Spirituality & Workplace Stress Management. Al- Adwa, 1-10.
  * Mir, U.R, Hassan, S.S. (2016). Caste and Entrepreneurial abilities: A contextual study of Sialkot city, Pakistan. Science International, 28(2), 1427-1429.
  * Qadri, M.M, Ayub, Umer & Mir, U.R. (2016).Globalization and Regionalization: At a glance on debate in pursuit of guiding principles leading policy implications. Journal of Management & Research, 3(2), 1-32.
