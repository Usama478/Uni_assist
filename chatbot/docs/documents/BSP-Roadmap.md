**Bridge Program for BSc Degree Holder Students**
**Bridge courses****:**
On the basis of various combination of subjects in BSc, Bridging courses will be offered in three different groups in accordance with their BSc subject combinations.
**Group 1**
Physics with Math (A&B)
**Group 2**
Physics, General Math, any combination (other than math) 
**Group 3**
Physics any combination (other than Math)
It is compulsory to have Physics as a Major in BSc Physics.
