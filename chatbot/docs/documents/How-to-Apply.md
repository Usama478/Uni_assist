## How To Apply Online
  * Signup on UMT admissions portal (<https://sktonline.umt.edu.pk/>) and get your password.
  * Sign in with your email address and password, and complete your application.
  * An application processing fee of Rs. 2000/- has to be paid in HBL (Habib Bank Limited) by using the cash deposit slip available in HBL banks. Please write your VPN on that deposit slip if using HBL deposit slip. Bank voucher is also attached in the application for your convenience.
  * After paying the processing fee, upload the paid bank voucher student copy in the processing fee section of your application.
  * Fields mentioned with (*) are mandatory to fill.
  * After filling each section, kindly click the ‘Save and proceed’ button available at the bottom of each page. You will have the facility to save the filled information and return later to complete and submit the application.
  * Kindly note, once submitted, the application will not be editable.
  * Once you will qualify for admission, you will be required to bring copies of all your educational documents, one CNIC copy, 8 passport size photographs and copy of second part roll number slip if result is awaited/Statement of entry in case of A-Level. Original educational documents will also be seen at the time of collecting admission fee voucher. 


## How to Apply
  * Get the Admission Kit that is available with the Office of Admissions for Rs 2000/-
  * Outstation candidates can receive the Admission Kit by sending a bank draft/money order of Rs 2100/- in favor of UMT.
  * Fill the Application Form provided with the Kit.
  * Submit the Form on or before the deadline.
  * Enclose the following material with the Form: 
    * Four photographs of size 1" x 1”. From amongst these three: 
      * Affix one photograph on the UMT Admit Card
      * Attach the remaining photographs with the Application Form.
    * Photocopy of National Identity Card or domicile.
    * Copies of academic records including mark sheets, degrees, certificates, diplomas, etc., duly attested by a gazetted officer or equivalent.
    * Provisional Certificate from the head of the institution, in case result is awaited.
    * Copy of the Roll No. Slip and a Provisional Certificate from a gazetted officer, in case of a private candidate.
    * Equivalence certificate from the Higher Education Commission of Pakistan in case of applicants with foreign qualifications.
    * Experience Certificate (if applicable).
