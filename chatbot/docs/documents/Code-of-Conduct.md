#  Office of Registrar (ORG)
Dear Graduating Participant,
Ibrahim Hasan Murad, President ILM Trust and UMT, Rector UMT, Director Campus UMT Sialkot, Chairman Surgikare, Board of Governors, Board of Trustees and the Faculty cordially invite you to the 3rd Convocation UMT Sialkot - 2021.
**Schedule – 3 rd CONVOCATION UMT SIALKOT **  
---  
**Rehearsal Day** |  **Convocation Day**  
**Day & Date** |  **Friday, November 05, 2021** |  **Day & Date** |  **Saturday, November 06, 2021**  
**Time:** |  **10:00 am** |  **Time:** |  **07:30 am**  
**Venue:** |  **Dastoor Event Complex, Ghazi Officers Colony, Ghazipur road, Sialkot Cantt.** |  **Venue:** |  **Dastoor Event Complex, Ghazi Officers Colony, Ghazipur road, Sialkot Cantt.**  
**Gown Distribution Schedule**
You may collect your **Gown, Convocation Card and Invitation Card** on the Rehearsal Day **From 11 AM to 01 PM.**
**Start of Rehearsal:** **2 PM**
You are advised to kindly bring your CNIC & payment receipt. 
Parents / siblings / friends are **NOT** allowed on the day of Rehearsal.
**Code of Conduct**
Please also note the following:
1) The participation in Convocation rehearsal is mandatory for all graduating participants.
2) Only parents are allowed along with Invitation card(s) on the day of Convocation.
3) Wearing a mask in the convocation premises is mandatory.
4) All graduating participants are expected to attend the Convocation in Formal / National dressing.
5) Please bring your original CNIC.
6) Smoking is strictly prohibited.
7) Each invitation card is valid for the entry of the one graduating participant’s parent only.
8) Bags/electronic gadgets /personal belongings are not allowed.
9) In preview of the security situation of the country, please extend full cooperation with the security personnel on duty.
**UMT Sialkot wishes you all the best for your future endeavours.**
