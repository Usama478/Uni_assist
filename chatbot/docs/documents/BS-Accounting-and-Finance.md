# Academic Programs
## BS Accounting and Finance
### Program Introduction
In today's fast-paced and challenging business era, finance and accounting are the major functions of every organization. BS Accounting and Finance program is designed to develop knowledge, expertise and skills which will enable a participant to cope successfully with the challenges of a rapidly evolving, global business and financial environment. The scope of accounting and finance is massive and opportunities are limitless.
The main objective of the program is to offer the distinctive opportunity to study the theory and practice relating to accounting and develop the proficiency in the application of accounting, auditing, taxation and finance principles. The program will enable the graduates to be absorbed in the ever demanding and rewarding field of accounting and finance.
### Program Details
Duration:
4 Years
Entry Requirements:
Inter / A Level or equivalent with 45% marks
