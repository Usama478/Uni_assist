# **_Request Form for Indoor Medical coverage_**

  - The addition request must be generated by the employee in accordance with the following timelines:
a) New addition of the employee (within 10 days of confirmation of service)
b) Spouse addition (within 20 days of marriage)
c) New Born dependent addition (within 20 days of birth)

  - Parents are **not covered** under Indoor Medical/Group Health Insurance Coverage

  - Son shall be covered **till 25 years of age**

  - Daughter shall be covered **till marriage**
