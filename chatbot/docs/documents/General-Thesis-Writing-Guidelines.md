# Knowledge Unit of Health Sciences
**Knowledge Unit of Health Sciences**  
Department of Physical Therapy and Rehabilitation
  
**General Thesis Writing Guidelines**  
**Doctor of Physiotherapy (DPT) Program**
  
**1. Thesis Length**
  
• Total thesis should be between **50 to 80 pages, excluding appendices**.  
• The word count should range from **12,000 to 20,000 words** ,  
=> excluding the abstract and references.  
• Page and word count may vary slightly depending on the nature of the study and formatting  
choices.
  
**2. Font and Formatting**
  
• Font: **Times New Romans**  
• Font Size: **12 pt** for body text; **14 pt bold** for main headings  
• Line Spacing: **1.5**  
• Margins: **1 inch** on all sides  
• Alignment: **Fully justified**  
• Page Numbering: **Bottom center** starting from Chapter 1
  
**3. Citation Style**
  
• Style: **Vancouver (Superscript format)**  
• Cite using superscript numbers after punctuation (e.g., “...is well-documented.1”)  
• References listed numerically in order of appearance at the end
  
**4. Referencing Requirements**
  
• Minimum References: 50  
• Recency: At least 70% of sources must be published within the last 5 to 10 years  
• Prefer peer-reviewed journal articles, evidence-based guidelines, and systematic reviews
  
**5. Language and Academic Writing**
  
• Use formal academic tone and third-person writing  
• Avoid personal pronouns (e.g., “I”, “we”)  
• Maintain clarity, precision, and grammatical accuracy
  
**6. Plagiarism Policy**
  
• Acceptable plagiarism limit: Below 19% (checked via Turnitin or equivalent)  
• All borrowed text, data, or visuals must be properly paraphrased and cited
  
**7. Tables and Figures**
  
• Each must have a number, title, and (if applicable) source citation  
• Titles above tables and figures; sources below  
• All must be referred to within the text (e.g., “see Figure 2”)
  
**8. Thesis Structure**
  
Preliminary Pages  
• Title Page  
• Dedication (optional)  
• Acknowledgments  
• Abstract (250–300 words)  
• Table of Contents  
• List of Tables  
• List of Figures  
• List of Abbreviations  
**Main Chapters**
  
**• Chapter 1: Introduction**
  
– Background  
– Problem Statement  
– Rationale  
– Objectives  
– Hypothesis/Research Questions
  
**• Chapter 2: Literature Review**
  
– Overview of existing research  
– Theoretical framework  
– Research gaps
  
**• Chapter 3: Methodology**
  
– Study design  
– Population and sampling  
– Inclusion/exclusion criteria  
– Data collection tools  
– Procedure  
– Ethical considerations  
– Statistical analysis plan
  
**• Chapter 4: Results**
  
– Data presentation (charts, tables, text)  
– No interpretation in this chapter  
  
**• Chapter 5: Discussion**
  
– Interpretation of findings  
– Comparison with existing studies  
– Implications, strengths, and limitations
  
**• Chapter 6: Conclusion and Recommendations**
  
– Key findings  
– Clinical/research recommendations  
– Future directions  
• **References** In Vancouver style, ordered numerically  
• **Appendices** Consent forms, questionnaire/tools, IRB approval, raw data (if applicable)
  
**9. Ethical Approval**
  
• Mandatory approval from the Institutional Review Board (IRB)  
• Must be attached in the appendix
  
**10. Thesis Submission and Defense**
  
• Submit 3 hardbound copies: For Supervisor, Internal Examiner, and External Examiner  
• Prepare a PowerPoint presentation (10–15 slides) for the viva voce/defense  
• Defense includes an oral presentation and question session with the examination panel
  
**11. Recommended Software Tools**
  
• Referencing: Zotero, EndNote, Mendeley  
• Plagiarism Check: Turnitin  
• Data Analysis: SPSS, Excel
  
**12. Hardbound Format**
  
• Cover Color: Blue  
• Text Embossing: Silver embossed text  
• Final thesis must be submitted in the standard university binding format
