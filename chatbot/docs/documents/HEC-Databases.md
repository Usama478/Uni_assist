# Learning Resource Center (LRC)
### HEC Digital Resources
UMT, Sialkot library has subscribed to several online digital databases to provide access to wide range of journals and eBooks related to various academic disciplines. Many e-Publications are made available by using this facility to provide campus-wide access. These databases will not only serve the UMT community but also the learning/research community and the society as a whole. We diligently ensure that said collection will benefit the present and future generations. We would make every effort to maximize the impact of these resources with careful planning and sensible utilization.
Note: Access to these high quality peer reviewed journals' databases and e-Books is provided to the University of Management and Technology through HEC National Digital Library Program. This IP address based access is available to you within University premises.
For further details please contact University Library Officials (Phone Ext: 130; E-mail address: lrc@skt.umt.edu.pk)
**Association of Computing Machinery (ACM) -**[**http://dl.acm.org/**](http://dl.acm.org/)  
---  
(Campus-Wide)  
  * The ACM contains full-text from 28 ACM Journals and Transactions, 10 ACM Magazines, over 40 ACM Special Interest Newsletters, 15 non-ACM journal and publications and over 100 annual conference proceedings.
  * Content strengths include all areas of Information Technology, with full archival content for all ACM publications.

  * The ASTM Standards & Engineering Digital Library is a vast collection of industry-leading standards and technical engineering information. It covers a broad range of engineering disciplines, including aerospace, biomedical, chemical, civil, environmental, geological, health and safety, industrial, materials science, mechanical, nuclear, petroleum, soil science and solar engine.

**The Institute for Operations Research and Management Sciences (INFORMS)** is the largest professional society in the world for professionals in the field of Operations Research. INFORMS publishes 15 scholarly journals that describe the latest OR/MS methods and applications and a magazine with news from across the profession.  
**JSTOR -**[**http://www.jstor.org/**](http://www.jstor.org/)  
---  
(Campus-Wide)  
JSTOR offers a high-quality, interdisciplinary archive to support scholarship and teaching. It includes archives of over 3000 leading core scholarly journals covering Business, Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, Language, Law, Religion, Philosophy, Anthropology, History, Geography, Biological Sciences, Political Sciences and Education, as well as selected monographs and other valuable materials. The entire corpus is full-text searchable. Key Features of JSTOR are: 1. More than 1,000 journal titles, 2. More than 4,000 participants, 3. More than 4 million articles, 4. More than 21 million pages, 5. Starts from volume 1 of each journal, 6. Issues scanned cover to cover.
