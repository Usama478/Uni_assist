# Learning Resource Center (LRC)
## E-Resources
UMT, Sialkot library has subscriptions to various electronic and online digital resources to provide access to wide range of online collections related to various academic disciplines. Many publications are being made available through this facility to provide access to e-databases of more than 30000 peer reviewed leading international journals. Users must use these resources under copyright laws.
As most of the full text material available in these databases is in PDF (Portable Document format). Users must have any PDF reader e.g. Adobe Acrobat reader, installed in their computers to view PDF files.
For further details/help regarding any problem encountered in accessing these databases, you may contact library officials through email at lrc@skt.umt.edu.pk, call at ext. 130 or visit library helpdesk.
