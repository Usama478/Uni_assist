## Shoaib Saleem
Lecturer
shoaibsaleem@umt.edu.pk
Mr. Shoaib Saleem is a highly accomplished professional with an impressive background in Accounting, finance and education. As a Chartered Management Accountant (ICMAP) with 10 years’ experience of capital markets and with 15 years of teaching experience to both Professional and academic students, he possesses a unique blend of practical expertise and exceptional pedagogical skills.
With his extensive Management Accounting, Financial Management, Investments and financial analysis knowledge, Mr. Shoaib brings a wealth of industry insights to the classroom.
Throughout his teaching career, Mr. Shoaib has consistently demonstrated a passion for empowering his students with the knowledge and skills necessary to excel in the field of Accounting & Finance. He has a proven track record of employing innovative teaching methodologies, combining theoretical concepts with real-world case studies to create a dynamic and engaging learning environment.
Mr. Shoaib ’s ability to break down complex financial concepts into easily understandable components has garnered him a reputation for being an approachable and effective educator. Students appreciate his dedication to their growth and development, as he actively fosters critical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and a strong foundation in subject area.
Beyond the classroom, Mr. Shoaib Saleem actively contributes to finance through professional engagements. He stays updated with the latest industry trends and regulations, ensuring that his teaching content remains relevant and up-to-date. Additionally, his strong network of industry connections provides valuable opportunities for his students to engage with finance professionals and gain practical exposure.
